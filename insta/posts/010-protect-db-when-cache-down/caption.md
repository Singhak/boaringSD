Your whole cache just died. With a 95% hit rate your DB suddenly gets 20x the load. Now what? 🚨

1️⃣ Prevent: replicas, failover, multi-AZ, persistence
2️⃣ Absorb: in-process L1 cache, serve stale, single-flight
3️⃣ Limit: rate limiting, load shedding, circuit breakers, connection pool caps
4️⃣ Degrade: defaults, feature flags, read-only mode
5️⃣ Recover: pre-warm hot keys and ramp traffic up slowly

Know your cache-miss capacity and run a game day with the cache OFF.
